## Accounting and privacy decisions to finish before implementation

1. **Separate credit principal from tax and collected total.** The selected tax-exclusive policy means a $10 selection adds $10 of credit and Stripe calculates additional tax. Verify `principal + tax = collected total` using checked exact integer arithmetic. Use supported Stripe Tax calculation, never locally guessed rates or the tax-inclusive total as credit.
2. **Canonical evidence must agree.** Verify the configured mode and automatic-tax completion, immutable original principal, canonical line item/subtotal, tax/total arithmetic, and Session → PaymentIntent → captured Charge agreement. Preserve partial-refund/dispute rejection, exact integer arithmetic and once-only funding. A signed event or browser return is not paid evidence. Tax zero is not proof of no collection obligation.
3. **Use the selected shared 20-day clock.** Changing `paymentSalesDays` from 90 to 20 is unsafe: current sale-date-based pruning can make a referenced sale eligible before the purchase's exact issuance-plus-20-day cutoff, and the purchase FK forbids that deletion. For the live schema, use the original purchase's exact `unlink_at`: remove the purchase and its dependent sale in the same supported transaction, respecting FK order and the combined bounded deletion budget. No additional sale-expiry timestamp or history is necessary. Do not extend retention, drop the FK or weaken replay admission; explicitly validate the one-purchase/one-sale invariant while retained.
4. **Minimal necessary records only.** Propose exact principal/tax/total fields and finite expiry metadata before persisting them. No addresses, customer tax IDs, names, emails, card details, raw provider objects, hosted URLs or tax location evidence in application records, diagnostics or telemetry. Stripe's own required collection/retention is a separately disclosed processor boundary. Twenty days is an operator-selected local policy, not a legal retention conclusion.

## Supported contract research and proposed live schema

Keep invoice creation and future-use saving off. Customer details can still enter transient SDK responses and Stripe retains them independently; do not persist those details locally. Actual hosted behavior remains a qualification gate.
- [Stripe price tax behavior](https://docs.stripe.com/tax/products-prices-tax-codes-tax-behavior): explicit `exclusive` adds tax to the fixed subtotal. Parent separately checked these official passages. Pinned v87.0.0 provides `AutomaticTax.Enabled`, inline `TaxBehavior` and product `TaxCode`, canonical automatic-tax status/liability, Session `AmountSubtotal`/`AmountTotal`/`TotalDetails.AmountTax`, and line-item subtotal/tax/total fields.
- Require tax completion at funding, not initial open-Session creation: location/tax may remain unresolved until Checkout is completed. Reject wrong live/test/direct context, wrong tax behavior/code, nonzero shipping/discount and non-complete tax evidence. Canonical PaymentIntent and captured Charge must equal the final collected total. Zero tax is valid only with completed canonical tax evidence, not proof of legal non-taxability.
- [Stripe Tax setup](https://docs.stripe.com/tax/set-up) and [zero-tax explanations](https://docs.stripe.com/tax/zero-tax) distinguish payment-account activation from registrations, classification and collection. Prepaid-credit/service classification and purchase-versus-redemption tax timing are operator/professional decisions; do not invent a product tax code. Require explicitly selected product-tax configuration before live admission. Standard calculation does not itself establish filing/remittance.
- [Stripe reports](https://docs.stripe.com/tax/reports) remain a separate provider/accounting record boundary. Do not claim the local 20-day choice establishes legal sufficiency or deletes those records.

Final canonical funding evidence supplies validated principal/tax/total. - Require explicitly configured live credentials, canonical HTTPS return origin and an operator-selected supported product tax code. Constructor/runtime validation performs no inference or payment mutation. Signed webhook is the funding trigger; status/return remain observational.
